WELCOME & SAFETY

Community guidelines

A supportive place for people of all digital experience levels to learn safer habits online.

1. Be kind and patient

Use respectful language. Different people have different levels of experience. Encourage questions and practical learning.

2. Protect everyone's privacy

Never post passwords, access codes, phone numbers, financial details, full addresses, identification documents or another person's private information. Moderators may hide unsafe posts.

3. Avoid harmful links and scams

Do not publish suspicious links, requests for payment, malware or misleading information. Describe a suspicious message without copying active phishing links.

4. Share helpful and accurate information

When possible, explain why you recommend an action. Forum replies are peer support, not professional financial or cybersecurity incident-response services.

5. Help us keep the forum safe

Use the “Report” option on discussions or replies that might put others at risk. Moderators can review reports and hide content.

Privacy notice for this prototype

To operate the forum, we store your chosen username, password hash, posts, replies, reports and a security session cookie. We do not ask for an email address and do not include third-party advertising or analytics. Public posts are visible to visitors. The database is stored on the forum server. The operator must publish its own privacy policy, define retention periods and provide a contact channel before public launch.

You can delete your member account and the contributions linked to it from the account page. If you need help with account rights, contact the project website's administrator.

Safety reminder

If you suspect an ongoing financial fraud or account takeover, contact your bank or service provider through its official channel. Do not share credentials on this forum.
